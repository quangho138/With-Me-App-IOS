# Sigh breathing audio

Source: **- Deep Breath**, recorded by **rrehl**.
https://freesound.org/people/rrehl/sounds/717167/

License: CC0 1.0 — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Adaptations: human inhale/exhale recording trimmed into cues, modest pitch-preserving tempo changes, fades, mono conversion and silence padding. The 14-second track has inhale cues at 0, 4 and 5 seconds and an exhale cue at 6 seconds. No ocean or synthetic noise layers.

Rebuild: `python tool/make_sigh_audio.py` (requires imageio-ffmpeg).
